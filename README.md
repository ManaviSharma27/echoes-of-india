# Echoes of India

LOKVAANI — AI-Powered Living Archive of India's Oral Heritage

Build a beautiful, premium, immersive, responsive web application called “LOKVAANI” — an AI-powered living digital archive dedicated to preserving, understanding, connecting and discovering India's oral cultural heritage.

BRAND

LOKVAANI

Tagline:
“Preserving voices. Connecting cultures.”

Core philosophy:

Preserve → Understand → Connect → Discover

LOKVAANI should feel like a living digital museum of India, not a generic SaaS dashboard, government portal, CMS, or AI chatbot.

The experience should make users feel that they are entering India's cultural memory.

1. PROJECT PURPOSE

LOKVAANI preserves and makes discoverable India's living oral heritage:

Folk stories

Folk songs

Oral histories

Traditional knowledge

Regional languages and dialects

Festivals and rituals

Traditional practices

Community memories

Cultural wisdom

The core technological pipeline is:

🎙️ Audio / Story
↓
🤖 AI Transcription
↓
🌐 Translation
↓
🏷️ Cultural Context & Metadata
↓
🔎 Semantic Search
↓
🧬 Cultural Connections
↓
🌏 Discovery

This is a Smart India Hackathon prototype/demo, not a production system.

Use realistic mock data and simulated AI processing. Every major frontend interaction should work convincingly without requiring a real backend.
